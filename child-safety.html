Child Safety & CSAE Standards

Mission Udyojak App

Our commitment: Mission Udyojak Foundation maintains a zero-tolerance approach to child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE) and child sexual abuse material (CSAM).

Last Updated: September 24, 2026

1. Zero-Tolerance Policy

Mission Udyojak Foundation is committed to protecting children and maintaining a safe environment for all users of the Mission Udyojak application.

We strictly prohibit:

  • Child sexual abuse or exploitation.
  • Grooming or attempts to establish inappropriate sexual relationships with minors.
  • Sexual exploitation or trafficking of children.
  • Sexual solicitation of minors.
  • Sextortion or coercion involving children.
  • Creating, uploading, requesting, sharing, storing, or distributing CSAM.
  • Any other activity or content that sexually exploits, abuses, or endangers children.

2. Prohibited Content and Conduct

Any content or conduct that facilitates, promotes, depicts, solicits, or enables child sexual abuse or exploitation is prohibited on or through Mission Udyojak.

Where Mission Udyojak obtains actual knowledge of prohibited activity, we will take appropriate action consistent with these standards, applicable law, and relevant platform requirements. This may include removing or restricting access to content and restricting or terminating accounts involved in prohibited activity.

3. Reporting Child Safety Concerns

Users who become aware of suspected CSAE, CSAM, grooming, exploitation, or another child-safety concern involving Mission Udyojak should report it immediately.

Child Safety / CSAE Contact:
Mission Udyojak Foundation
Email: missionudyojakapp@gmail.com

Please provide enough information for us to understand and investigate the report. Do not intentionally send or redistribute illegal child sexual abuse material by email or through any other channel.

4. User Feedback and Reporting

Mission Udyojak provides a mechanism for users to communicate concerns and report inappropriate activity. Users can contact our support team at:

missionudyojakapp@gmail.com

Reports concerning child safety are treated seriously and reviewed for appropriate action.

5. Enforcement

If we identify activity that violates these standards, we may take appropriate enforcement action, including:

  • Removing or restricting prohibited content.
  • Restricting, suspending, or terminating the relevant account.
  • Permanently terminating an account where appropriate.
  • Preserving relevant information where legally required.
  • Reporting matters to appropriate authorities or organizations where required by applicable law.

6. Compliance With Child Safety Laws

Mission Udyojak Foundation is committed to complying with applicable child-safety, online-safety, and child-protection laws and regulations.

Where applicable law requires reporting of confirmed CSAM or other child exploitation, we will follow the applicable reporting requirements and cooperate with competent authorities.

7. Child Safety Point of Contact

The following contact is designated to receive child-safety concerns and communications relating to CSAE prevention and enforcement:

Mission Udyojak Foundation
Email: missionudyojakapp@gmail.com

8. Cooperation With Authorities

Where required by applicable law, Mission Udyojak Foundation may cooperate with law enforcement, child-protection authorities, or other competent organizations regarding suspected child abuse or exploitation.

9. Updates to These Standards

We may update these Child Safety & CSAE Standards from time to time to reflect changes in our services, applicable laws, or platform requirements. The latest version will be published on this page.
